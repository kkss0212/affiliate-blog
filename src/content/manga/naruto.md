Naruto opens on a genuinely bleak premise for a Shonen Jump series: its
title character is a village outcast, shunned by nearly everyone around
him for reasons he doesn't understand as a child, chasing recognition as
much as strength. Masashi Kishimoto, born in the small town of Nagi in
Okayama Prefecture, ran the series in Weekly Shonen Jump from 1999 to
2014, and that outsider-seeking-belonging structure stayed the emotional
core even as the story expanded into a sprawling ninja-world political
epic across 700 chapters.

### An outcast hero, and the comparison that follows him

Naruto's most consistent international framing is a direct comparison to
Harry Potter, and it's not an accidental one: both stories center a
destined outsider hero, build a core trio with complementary skills and
real friction between them (Naruto-Sakura-Sasuke sits in almost the exact
same shape as Harry-Hermione-Ron), and lean heavily on found-family
relationships filling in for absent or complicated biological ones. The
parallel has come up often enough in Western coverage that Kishimoto has
addressed it directly in interviews, generally as a comparison he
understands rather than one he set out to invite.

### A twin-brother coincidence in the same industry

One detail that tends to surprise readers: Kishimoto has an identical twin
brother, Seishi Kishimoto, who is also a professional manga artist,
best known for the series 700 Seas and O-Parts Hunter. It's a genuinely
unusual coincidence in an industry built almost entirely around solo,
individually distinctive creators — two twins from the same small Okayama
town, both ending up as working mangaka.

## Where to start

Volume 1, "Uzumaki Naruto," is the right place to begin — it's paced as a
slow build into the larger ninja-world plot, so the first few volumes read
more like a straightforward coming-of-age story than the political epic
the series eventually becomes. If you'd rather watch first, the original
2002 Naruto anime covers the same early material before the story moves
into its Shippuden continuation.
